# PRD: Personal Inventory and Stock Management

- **Status:** Implemented and locally validated on `feat/stock-keeping`; existing dependency audit findings and hosted rollout remain documented in the Inventory Module guide
- **Owner:** Ashley
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-05
- **Related documents:** [Inventory Module](../INVENTORY_MODULE.md), [Finance PRD](PRD_007.md), [Finance Module](../FINANCE_MODULE.md)

## Summary

Create an Inventory module for personal essentials, including hair care, skincare, and household supplies such as tissues. Use a simplified supermarket inventory structure: create products once, receive purchases through a shopping cart, add them to a shelf, and track consumption. Show unopened quantities, estimated unopened stock duration, and historical price comparisons, with optional links to existing Finance expenses afterward.

Ashley authorized implementation of the complete PRD on 2026-10-05. The implementation decisions below resolve the earlier draft questions within that scope.

## Problem

The user needs to know what essentials they already own, how much unopened stock remains, and how long that stock typically lasts. Repeat purchases may have different sizes, pack quantities, or prices, making it difficult to tell whether a new price is cheaper or more expensive than usual. Inventory entry should remain independent of Finance entry while allowing the records to be associated later.

## Goals

- Create each product once and reuse it for future stock additions.
- Receive several purchased products through one shopping-cart-style flow.
- Show unopened quantities by product, with size and pack breakdowns available on hover or tap.
- Keep opened items visible separately without counting them as full remaining stock.
- Learn consumption from start and finish dates, then estimate how long unopened stock lasts.
- Compare purchase prices using equivalent quantities across compatible sizes.
- Link saved purchases to existing Finance expenses without duplicating spending.

## Non-Goals

- Product photos or image storage.
- Barcode scanning.
- Storage locations.
- Shared household inventory in the initial version.
- Sales, checkout payments, or customer management.
- Manual tracking of partially remaining contents.
- Predicted run-out dates for opened items.
- Sufficiency judgements, minimum-stock rules, restock alerts, or automatic purchasing.
- Automatic creation of Finance expenses.

## Users and Access

| User or role | Allowed actions |
|---|---|
| Authenticated owner with Inventory access | Manage their products, stock, purchases, usage history, and adjustments. |
| Same owner with Finance access | Link an inventory purchase to an existing Finance expense they can access. |
| Unauthenticated visitor or another user | No access to the owner's inventory records. |

The initial experience is for the owner's personal use. Shared inventory and household collaboration are outside scope.

## User Flow

### Create a Product

1. Open the product catalogue and select **Add product**.
2. Enter its name, optional brand, category, and tracking unit.
3. Add a size or pack variant, such as a 500 ml bottle or a pack of five tissue boxes.
4. Save the product without needing to receive stock immediately.
5. Reuse the product and its variants when buying more. Add another size when needed.

### Receive Stock

1. Open **Add stock**.
2. Select an existing product or create a new one.
3. Select its size or pack variant.
4. Enter the quantity purchased and price paid.
5. Add other products to the receiving cart.
6. Review the cart and its purchase date.
7. Confirm **Add to shelf**.
8. Optionally open the saved purchase later and link it to an existing Finance expense.

Each cart line allows an explicit choice between price per purchased unit and total line price. The purchase date applies to the whole cart. Users can edit or remove lines before confirmation, and stock changes only after confirmation.

| Item | Quantity purchased | Line total | Added to shelf |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Shampoo, 500 ml | 2 bottles | RM36 | 1,000 ml, 2 bottles |
| Moisturiser, 50 g | 1 jar | RM25 | 50 g, 1 jar |
| Tissues, 5-box pack | 2 packs | RM30 | 10 boxes |
| **Total** | | **RM91** | |

### Use an Item

1. Select a product and variant from My Shelf.
2. Mark one usable item as **Start using**.
3. The item leaves unopened stock and appears separately as in use, with a recorded start date.
4. Mark that item as **Finished** when consumed.
5. Its completed usage record becomes available for stock-duration calculations.

For tissues, the usable item is one box. Opening the outer multipack does not start usage; starting one box removes one box from unopened stock.

### Product Catalogue

Each product contains a required name, optional brand, category, tracking unit, and one or more size or pack variants. Supported tracking units cover volume, weight, and count.

| Product | Variants | Shelf unit |
|---|---|---|
| Dove Shampoo | 250 ml bottle, 500 ml bottle | ml |
| Moisturiser | 50 g jar, 100 g jar | g |
| Tissues | Single box, pack of 5 boxes | boxes |

Variants preserve the difference between package size and item identity. Tissue boxes with different contents remain distinguishable even when both are counted as boxes. Optional sheets per item is supported for countable variants and separates comparable price and usage groups.

### My Shelf

My Shelf is the main Inventory dashboard and uses a compact text-based layout without photos. Each product displays its name, total unopened quantity, in-use count, approximate unopened stock duration when available, and quick actions for **Add stock** and **Start using**.

Example with illustrative usage history:

> **Dove Shampoo**
>
> 1,250 ml unopened · 1 bottle in use
>
> Approximately 100 days of unopened stock

Hovering over or tapping the stock quantity reveals:

- 2 × 500 ml unopened.
- 1 × 250 ml unopened.
- 1 × 500 ml in use.

The full product view provides purchase history, price comparisons, usage history, and stock adjustments. Essential details must also be accessible without hover, including on mobile and by keyboard.

Aggregate compatible units within each product. Do not combine unrelated products or mix ml, g, and counts into one quantity total.

### Usage States

| State | Meaning | Included in unopened totals? |
|---|---|---|
| Unopened | Available on the shelf | Yes |
| In use | Opened and being consumed | No |
| Finished | Fully consumed | No |

Discarded, lost, or given-away items are recorded through stock adjustments. An in-use item discarded before completion must not be treated as normally finished.

### Unopened Stock Duration

The estimate answers how long the unopened stock would last based on recorded consumption. It does not estimate the contents or run-out date of an opened item.

Example:

- A completed 500 ml shampoo bottle lasted 40 days.
- The observed rate is 12.5 ml per day.
- There is 1,000 ml of unopened shampoo.
- Estimated unopened stock duration is 80 days.

For measured products, use compatible quantities such as ml or g. For countable products, use comparable usable units such as tissue boxes. Label estimates as approximate and make their calculation basis available in product details.

Exclude items removed through adjustments from normal consumption history. Display **No usage estimate yet** when history is missing or unsuitable. Keep different formulations as separate products and incompatible countable contents in separate comparison groups. Use all completed comparable records: divide consumed quantity by the union of their recorded calendar-day intervals, counting overlaps once and excluding gaps. Same-day completion contributes one day. If any stocked group lacks usable history, omit the overall duration estimate.

### Purchase History and Price Comparison

Every purchase retains its date, product, variant, quantity purchased, total paid, price per usable item, comparable unit price, and optional Finance link.

The price comparison shows the prospective price, most recent purchase price, usual purchase price, lowest recorded comparable price, and percentage difference from the usual price when those values are available.

| Product type | Comparison basis |
|---|---|
| Liquids | Price per 100 ml |
| Products measured by weight | Price per 100 g |
| Countable supplies | Price per usable unit, such as a box |

For example, RM18 for 500 ml equals RM3.60 per 100 ml, while RM10 for 250 ml equals RM4.00 per 100 ml. Tissue variants with different contents remain identifiable because price per box alone does not establish equivalent value.

Calculate the usual price as total recorded spend divided by total comparable quantity purchased, across all recorded history. Larger purchases receive proportionate weight. Only known MYR prices in comparable units contribute; the initial version follows Finance's MYR currency.

### Finance Integration

Inventory purchases remain complete and usable without a Finance link. Selecting an expense opens a purchase price review. Only Save purchase commits the chosen prices and link together, without creating or editing a Finance expense.

Several inventory items from one shopping trip can share the expense. The expense can include other purchases that are not tracked in Inventory, so the totals do not need to match. Removing a link must not delete stock, the inventory purchase, or the Finance expense.

### Stock Adjustments

Support quantity corrections, discarded or damaged items, losses, and gifts. Each adjustment retains the product, variant, quantity, date, and reason. Keep adjustments visible in stock history and prevent negative stock.

### Initial Stock Setup

Allow existing belongings to be added without requiring their historical purchase date or price.

- Existing unopened items contribute to shelf quantities.
- Existing opened items appear as in use.
- Unknown prices do not contribute to price comparisons.
- Unknown usage start dates do not produce invented consumption durations.

## Data and Interfaces

The implementation uses the following owner-scoped records; see the Inventory Module document for table names and constraints:

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product | Reusable identity, name, brand, category, and tracking unit. |
| Variant | Size, usable unit, and purchase-pack quantity under a product. |
| Purchase and purchase lines | One confirmed receiving cart and its item-level quantity and price history. |
| Stock batch | Stock originating from a purchase line or a supported initial-stock entry. |
| Usage record | An individual usable item's start date, finish date, and link to its stock batch. |
| Adjustment | A dated stock correction or removal with a reason. |
| Finance link | Optional association between a saved inventory purchase and an existing Finance expense. |

All records belong to the owner. Preserve purchase and usage history when stock reaches zero. The Inventory parent opens the shelf (`/inventory`); its sidebar submodules are Purchases (`/inventory/purchases`) and Usage history (`/inventory/usage`), all inheriting Inventory access, with session-only `/api/inventory` read and mutation endpoints and `/api/inventory/expenses` for Finance selection. Register the `inventory` module in the database catalogue and canonical route mapping, with an owner-role grant and the existing override rules.

Receiving confirmation must be atomic and safe to retry. Stock actions must also prevent duplicate transitions, such as starting the same item twice or finishing it twice.

## Security and Privacy

- Require the existing authenticated session and module access controls.
- Enforce ownership for product, purchase, stock, usage, and adjustment reads and writes.
- Require Finance access and permission to the selected expense when creating a Finance link.
- Use existing application authorization patterns when implementation is approved.
- No image storage or barcode data is required by this scope.

## Failure and Edge Cases

| Scenario | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| Empty cart or invalid quantity | Require valid cart lines before receiving stock. |
| Receipt save fails or the response is interrupted | Do not leave partially received stock; a retry must not duplicate a completed receipt. |
| Product has no stock | Keep the product and its history available for future purchases. |
| No suitable completed usage history | Show quantity and "No usage estimate yet." |
| Several items are used concurrently | Track each item separately and count overlapping completed usage days once when calculating consumption rates. |
| Selected stock was already used or adjusted | Reject an invalid transition and refresh the available quantity. |
| Catalogue size or pack details change | Preserve historical purchase quantities and prices. |
| Finance expense includes other shopping items | Allow linking without forcing inventory and expense totals to match. |
| Finance expense is unavailable or access is denied | Keep the inventory purchase intact and report the linking issue. |
| An item is discarded before being finished | Record an adjustment and exclude it from normal completed usage. |
| Comparable price history is unavailable or its baseline is zero | Show the available prices without an invalid percentage comparison. |

## Implementation Decisions

- **Initial stock:** Support optional historical dates and prices, and already opened items with unknown start dates. Unknown values never create invented price or usage history.
- **Usage estimates:** Use all comparable completed history, merge overlapping intervals, exclude gaps, and estimate unopened quantities only. Do not produce run-out dates or restock recommendations.
- **Usual price:** Use quantity-weighted comparable MYR prices across all recorded history.
- **Tissue detail:** Support optional sheets per item. Compare specified sizes only with matching sizes and keep unspecified contents in their own group.
- **Scope authorization:** The explicit implementation request on 2026-10-05 supersedes the earlier draft-only instruction. Hosted deployment remains a separate rollout step.

### Product classification

Products have a required category and an optional subcategory, separate from their product name. For example, Hair Care > Shampoo > Intensive Repair Shampoo. Subcategory choices are scoped to the selected category and users can add new names. Changing category clears the subcategory. Inventory filters support both levels, and existing products remain valid without a subcategory.

### Purchase corrections and confirmations

Purchases and product history expose Edit purchase. Users can correct the stock source, date, quantities and line totals across the complete receipt. Existing-stock dates and prices can remain unknown. Corrections retain original size/pack snapshots, usage records, adjustments and Finance links. Quantity differences update unopened stock atomically; invalid chronology, negative resulting stock and stale revisions are rejected. Successful saves use the same dismissible, automatically expiring toast as Finance.

All Inventory action notifications, including failures and loading errors, use shared toasts. Keep loading progress, empty states and field-specific validation within the relevant UI. Dismissing a failure must not remove the retry action or entered form data.

Purchase cards place editing and Finance link actions in a three-dot menu and omit visible Finance-linked status text.

The receiving cart groups the price amount and its Per item / Per pack or Total selector on one row, including mobile layouts.

### Review prices when linking Finance

After choosing a Finance expense, open the purchase editor. Prefill the line total for single-line purchases; for multiple lines, retain prices and display the expense amount as an allocation reference. Save prices and the selected link together only on Save purchase. Cancel changes neither. Allow differing totals and never change the Finance expense. Recheck ownership and access and reject stale purchase revisions.
